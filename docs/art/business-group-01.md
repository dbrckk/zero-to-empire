# AAA Business Art — Group 01

Locked visual direction for the first four economy assets.

## Shared rules
- Isometric / three-quarter silhouette, readable at 50–128 dp.
- Dark premium base materials, strong emissive accent, warm practical lights.
- Seven presentation states: locked, base, Lv25, Lv100, Lv250, Lv500, Lv1000/Mastered.
- Idle motion must remain subtle and must respect Android reduced-motion and power-save policies.
- Upgrade feedback uses construction/energy bursts rather than UI-only flashes.
- Mastered state must change silhouette, not only add glow.

## 01 — Street Stand
- Identity: humble street food / market cart, striped canopy, wooden counter, wheels, crates, lantern.
- Accent: emerald green + warm amber practical light.
- Evolution: extra stock -> reinforced canopy -> illuminated sign -> premium cart -> energy awning -> mastered golden-green market shrine.

## 02 — Corner Shop
- Identity: compact neighborhood storefront, awning, windows, roof sign, street lamp.
- Accent: electric blue + warm interior light.
- Evolution: signage -> larger glazing -> rooftop equipment -> second level -> holographic sign -> mastered flagship store.

## 03 — Workshop
- Identity: artisan/industrial workshop, chimney, forge light, pipes, tools and compact machinery.
- Accent: furnace orange + graphite steel.
- Evolution: tooling -> chimney -> machine annex -> reinforced roof -> automated forge -> mastered high-output atelier.

## 04 — Factory
- Identity: heavy industrial plant, multiple stacks, tanks, pipes, central production hall.
- Accent: royal violet/magenta + cold steel.
- Evolution: extra stack -> tanks -> side production wing -> power conduits -> automated towers -> mastered neon industrial complex.

## Animation language
- Street Stand: canopy flutter, lantern pulse, tiny steam plume.
- Corner Shop: window light sweep, sign pulse, subtle door/window activity.
- Workshop: gear rotation, forge breathing, chimney smoke.
- Factory: stack exhaust, conduit pulses, turbine/pipe energy cycles.

## Special beats
- Unlock: vertical materialization + ground light.
- Purchase: short construction spark/burst.
- Milestone: accent shockwave + silhouette attachment reveal.
- Mastery: gold-white crown light, persistent premium idle layer.
