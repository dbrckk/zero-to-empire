# Power Core — AAA Evolution

The Power Core is the visual anchor of Empire Tycoon. It evolves by era, not only by particle count.

## Era progression
1. Ground / Startup — gold mechanical core, cross-brace motif.
2. Industrial / Orbital — cyan-violet reactor shell and articulated spokes.
3. Planetary — brighter orbital energy architecture.
4. Stellar — solar plasma core with orbiting collectors.
5. Galactic — luminous stellar containment.
6. Intergalactic — portal-like ring machinery.
7. Cosmic — layered dimensional rings and spectral core.
8. Reality Engine — counter-rotating elliptical field geometry.
9. Reality fracture — dimensional diamond frame and causal lattice.
10. Transcendent — white-gold singularity, violet secondary field, radiant nexus rays.

## Motion
- Slow structural rotation plus faster energy trails.
- Pulse synchronized with core bloom.
- Orbital node counts increase with progression.
- Reduced Motion freezes all continuous rotation while preserving the final composition.
- Power Save reduces trail samples, orbiters and expensive rays.

## Production requirements
- Distinct silhouette at every major era family.
- Never depend on emoji or raster fallback.
- Must remain readable at HUD scale.
- Maintain visual continuity with business palettes.
- High-tier states should feel radically more powerful without turning into unreadable particle noise.
