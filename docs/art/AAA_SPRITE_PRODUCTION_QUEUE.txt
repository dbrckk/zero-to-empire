ZERO → EMPIRE — AAA SPRITE PRODUCTION QUEUE
Version: 1.0
Status: source-of-truth companion to AAA_WORLD_SPRITE_BIBLE.md
Goal: replace procedural placeholder art progressively with authored, layered 2.5D production assets without changing gameplay IDs, economy, save schema or monetization.

1. CURRENT ART STATUS

The current Ascendant City is a playable visual prototype built mostly with Jetpack Compose primitives:
- Canvas paths, rectangles, ovals, lines and gradients for roads, foundations, pipes, skyline and shadows.
- BusinessArtIcon for business silhouettes/evolution art.
- EraVistaAAA for background atmosphere.
- Compose text/surfaces for HUD and interaction labels.

This is intentionally a structural prototype. It is NOT the final AAA asset pipeline. The final look must progressively replace placeholder/procedural building bodies with authored layered sprite masters and sprite sheets while keeping cheap procedural effects for glows, sparks, rings and traffic trails.

2. ORIGINAL WORLD ART TARGET

World: The Ascendant City.
Visual fantasy: scrap yard → industrial district → commercial city → automation capital → vertical megacity → hypercity → orbital economy → planetary network → quantum dominion → stellar empire → singularity crown.
Perspective: consistent portrait-mobile 2.5D, elevated three-quarter view, approximately 30–38 degree camera elevation.
Lighting: upper-left warm key, cool environment fill, selective emissive machinery.
Rule: dark material mass + selective energy accents. Never turn the whole world into neon.
Reference quality principle: readable, lively tycoon world at first glance, but all architecture, characters, props, layouts and silhouettes remain original to ZERO → EMPIRE.

4. MASTER FILE CONTRACT

For every business/tier generate or author a 2048x2048 transparent master.
Subject footprint: max 80% of canvas.
Transparent safe padding: minimum 8% each side.
Pivot: bottom-center of ground footprint.
No text, currency, UI, logos or level numbers baked into art.
No rectangular background.
Contact shadow is a separate layer.
Perspective and lighting must remain identical across all tiers of the same business.

6. FRAME/SLICING CONTRACT

Major building frame: 512x512.
Character/vehicle frame: 256x256.
Small FX frame: 128x128.
Minimum transparent inter-frame padding: 4 px.
Identical pivot in every frame.
No per-frame trim.
Order frames left-to-right, then top-to-bottom.

Default budgets:
idle micro-loop: 4–8 frames, 6–10 fps.
production action: 8–16 frames, 10–15 fps.
worker walk: 6–10 frames, 10–14 fps.
construction/evolution one-shot: 10–20 frames or procedural tween.
hero event: maximum 24 frames only when memory budget allows.

13. QUALITY GATES BEFORE COMMITTING ART

Every generated asset must pass all gates:
A. Originality: no recognizable copied building, character or UI from another game.
B. Silhouette: readable when previewed around 88 dp.
C. Perspective: same camera family as existing world.
D. Lighting: coherent with era.
E. Layerability: moving parts visually separable.
F. Pivot: bottom-center stable.
G. Alpha: clean transparent edges, no accidental background matte.
H. Compression: no visible halo/banding after Android packaging.
I. Motion: frame-to-frame volume and pivot stable.
J. Performance: texture size appropriate; no gratuitous large sheets.
K. Reduced motion: static frame remains visually complete.
L. Gameplay safety: art contains no economy logic or save changes.

15. INTEGRATION STRATEGY

Do not replace every procedural asset at once.
Use progressive replacement:
- Keep current procedural Ascendant City as fallback.
- Add authored sprites one business/tier at a time.
- If an authored asset is unavailable, route to existing BusinessArtIcon.
- Keep Compose/Canvas for cheap glows, shadows, conduits, sparks, income pops and transition rings.
- Use authored PNG/layer assets for the expensive visual information: architecture, machinery, characters and distinctive vehicles.
- Later introduce deterministic sprite metadata so slicing and pivots are data-driven instead of hard-coded.

16. DEFINITION OF AAA FOR THIS PROJECT

AAA-like here does not mean console-scale polygon count. It means every visible element has intentional art direction, coherent materials, strong silhouette, layered motion, tactile feedback, readable hierarchy, stable performance, polished transitions and a world that physically communicates progression.

A screen full of gradients and cards is not sufficient.
A screen full of random generated PNGs is not sufficient either.
The target is a coherent authored world whose buildings, characters, props, lighting, animation timing and UI all look as if they belong to the same production pipeline.
