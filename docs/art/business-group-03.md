# Business Group 03 — AAA Art Direction

## Scope
- 8 — Dyson Network
- 9 — Galactic Exchange
- 10 — Intergalactic Gateway
- 11 — Cosmic Foundry

## Visual progression
Base → Lv25 → Lv100 → Lv250 → Lv500 → Lv1000+/Mastered.

## Dyson Network
- Palette: stellar gold / white-hot plasma.
- Identity: captured star, segmented collector swarm, orbital relays.
- Motion: slow collector orbit, stellar pulse, relay traffic.
- Mastery: corona shell, outer crown ring, white-hot nodes.

## Galactic Exchange
- Palette: cyan / violet / warm trade beacons.
- Identity: orbital trade hub, rotating lanes, central exchange tower.
- Motion: traffic dots, orbit lanes, holographic trading arcs.
- Mastery: full exchange halo and high-density route network.

## Intergalactic Gateway
- Palette: violet / cyan / white aperture.
- Identity: monumental portal ring with stabilizer pylons.
- Motion: aperture breathing, counter-rotating nodes, portal arcs.
- Mastery: reinforced crown, white singularity aperture and navigation stars.

## Cosmic Foundry
- Palette: magenta / gold / deep-space steel.
- Identity: cosmic forge converting stellar matter into structures.
- Motion: furnace core pulse, matter nodes, energy feed lines.
- Mastery: forge crown, white sparks, outer energy shell.

## Performance/accessibility
- Reduced Motion freezes procedural phase while preserving the final composition.
- Power Save reduces particle/node counts and orbit density.
- No external licensed art dependency; renderer is procedural Compose Canvas.

`BusinessArtIcon` routes business IDs 8–11 through `BusinessGroup03Sprite`.
